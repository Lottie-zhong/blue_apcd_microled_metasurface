# Dipole-TMM to 2D-FDTD residual contract v1

The residual denotes model discrepancy plus numerical/device-domain difference. It is not an absolute-power residual or Purcell claim. Formal angular diagnostics use FDTD filter=0.2; filter=0 is audit-only. Ranking agreement/disagreement is recorded separately by metric; no champion or promotion decision is made.
