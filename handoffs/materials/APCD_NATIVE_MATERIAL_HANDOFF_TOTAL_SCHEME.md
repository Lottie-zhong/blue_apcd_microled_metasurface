# APCD native material handoff for total scheme

LP/CP pillars and their K=6 descendants use APCD_TIO2_NATIVE_M1. MDC high-index layers use APCD_TIO2_NATIVE_M1; MDC low-index layers, SiO2 defect, and RCLED-MDC filter use APCD_SIO2_NATIVE_M1 as applicable. Future source-coupled FDTD must record material_reference_id. Historical n=2.25/n=2.50/n=2.60 outputs remain legacy only.
