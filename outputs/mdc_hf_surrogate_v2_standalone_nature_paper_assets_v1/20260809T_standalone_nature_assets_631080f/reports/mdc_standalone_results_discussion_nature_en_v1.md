# Standalone MDC results and discussion

The frozen M1 geometry-conditioned ensemble is useful as a ranking and screening instrument over the defined DOE96/Test40 interface. OOF comparison selected M1 before external Test40 inspection, while PCA32 was selected by held-out DOE96 cross-fit profile fidelity. On Test40, the frozen metrics quantify profile discrepancy and rank behavior; they do not establish absolute physical power calibration.

The MDC-to-NP coupling is therefore one-way: the surrogate identifies candidates and exposes a compact source-normalized profile interface, after which direct FDTD confirmation is required. The scope deliberately excludes HF15 labels, sealed tests, solver execution, retraining, and any reinterpretation of the frozen power head.
