# PW modal normalization contract

Raw complex Ex/Ey/Ez/Hx/Hy/Hz at MON_IN, MON_PRENP, and MON_POSTNP remain the authority. Floquet amplitudes use a deterministic vector TE/TM basis built from the local propagation vector and +z reference; the complex amplitude phase is referenced to the canonical physical plane after de-embedding.

Propagating modal power is obtained from the time-averaged Poynting flux of the normalized vector basis and is positive for +z/upward and negative for -z/downward before direction-specific reporting. Incident +z power at Z_IN_REF is the normalization. Evanescent amplitudes are retained as complex state values with their kz and decay convention, but receive no false propagating power.
